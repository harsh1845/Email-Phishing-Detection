PhishGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

PhishGuard inspects hyperlinks in Gmail messages you open, on your device, to warn about phishing.

What we collect

PhishGuard does not collect, transmit, or sell email content, sender addresses, or browsing history. Processing is local: link URLs, sender display name/email as on-device context, and domains you mark trusted (stored in your browser profile).

Optional network requests (off by default)

Live threat feeds fetch hostnames from URLhaus. Google Safe Browsing, if enabled with your API key, receives 4-byte SHA-256 hash prefixes — not full URLs or email. Safe Browsing is for non-commercial use; commercial use requires Web Risk.

Sharing

No ads, no analytics SDKs, no data sales. Uninstalling removes stored settings.
